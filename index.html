QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

A few answers before you start your project.

What services does Bossy Prints & Design offer?

We offer T-shirt printing, graphic design, business branding, banners, posters, stickers, labels, caps and custom merchandise.

Can I order a custom T-shirt?

Yes. Use our Start a Project form to tell us your quantity, sizes, colours, print position and other requirements.

Can you design my artwork?

Yes. We can work with your idea and create graphic designs suitable for printing and branding.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the Start a Project form on this website. Submit your requirements and we will review the project and contact you with the next steps and quotation.

Can I upload my own artwork?

Yes. You can upload PNG, JPG, JPEG, PDF or SVG artwork with your project submission.
