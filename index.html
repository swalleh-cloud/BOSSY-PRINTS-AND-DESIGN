Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I submit my own artwork?

Yes. You can upload your artwork when starting a project. We currently accept PNG, JPG, PDF and SVG files.

Can you design something for me?

Yes. Select Graphic Design or the relevant service and explain what you need in the project form.

Can I order only one item?

Yes, depending on the product and service. Tell us your quantity when submitting your project.

How do I get a quotation?

Submit your project details. We review the requirements and communicate the quotation with you.

Can I pick up my order?

Yes. Select Pickup when submitting your project. Delivery can also be requested where available.
